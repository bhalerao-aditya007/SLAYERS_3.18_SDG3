# Team Slayers

## Problem Statement
**S ID: 3.18 — Engineer software simulating drug interactions for personalized HIV treatment plans (B2B for pharmacies)**

Team Slayers is developing a software system aimed at improving the safety and precision of HIV treatment by simulating drug interactions in complex medication regimens. HIV patients often rely on multi-drug therapies, and the risk of harmful interactions increases when additional medications are introduced for comorbid conditions.

Our project focuses on building a pharmacy-oriented B2B solution that assists in evaluating treatment combinations, supporting informed decision-making, and reducing preventable adverse drug interactions. The goal is to contribute toward safer, more personalized HIV care through intelligent simulation and data-driven validation.
